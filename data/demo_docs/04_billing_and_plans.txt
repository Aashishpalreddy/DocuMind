CloudSync — Billing & Plans FAQ

What plans are available?
Free: 5GB storage, 1,000 API calls/month, 1 member — no credit card required.
Pro: $12/month, 100GB storage, 50,000 API calls/month, up to 10 workspace members.
Enterprise: custom pricing, custom storage/call limits, SSO, dedicated support.

How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and you're
charged a prorated amount for the rest of the billing cycle. Downgrades take effect
at the start of the next billing cycle.

What happens if I exceed my storage limit?
On the Free tier, uploads are blocked once you hit 5GB until you delete files or
upgrade. Pro and Enterprise tiers allow temporary overages billed at $0.10/GB, with
an email warning at 90% usage.

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the
end of the current billing period, after which your workspace reverts to the Free
tier limits (data is not deleted, but new uploads are blocked if over 5GB).

Do you offer refunds?
Refunds are issued for accidental charges within 14 days of the charge date. Contact
support with your invoice ID to request one. Refunds are not issued for partial
months after voluntary downgrades or cancellations.

Is there a student or nonprofit discount?
Yes, 50% off Pro tier for verified students and registered nonprofits. Apply at
cloudsync.example.com/discounts with proof of eligibility.

How is API usage counted toward my monthly limit?
Every call to `/sync/push`, `/sync/pull`, `/sync/status`, and `/workspaces` counts
as one API call. Webhook deliveries do not count against your limit.
